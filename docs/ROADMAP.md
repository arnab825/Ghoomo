# Ghoomo - Product Roadmap

## Phase 1: Hackathon MVP (Current Sprint)
- URL ingestion (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Blogs)
- Automatic place extraction with source traceability
- Confidence score indicators with manual correction fallback
- Interactive Leaflet map with dark theme & route polylines
- Proximity-based geographic clustering into daily itineraries
- Trip workspace with split map/itinerary view
- Link-based collaboration (Editor vs Viewer permissions)
- Expense budget calculator & packing checklist

## Phase 2: Post-Hackathon v1.1
- Real-time Supabase presence for simultaneous multi-user planning
- Smart pace selector (Relaxed / Balanced / Packed)
- Route optimization with real India road transit time estimations
- Shareable travel story cards for Instagram & WhatsApp status

## Phase 3: Commercial & Ecosystem Expansion
- Direct integration with verified local homestays and regional guides
- Weather intelligence for dynamic indoor/outdoor swaps
- Multilingual voice assistance for on-the-go ground navigation
