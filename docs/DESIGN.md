# Ghoomo - Design System & UI/UX Guidelines

## 1. Design Philosophy
- **Map-First**: The map is a primary visual anchor, not an afterthought buried beneath tables.
- **Inspiration-to-Plan Focused**: Fast input, instant visual feedback, zero boilerplate.
- **Modern & India-Aware**: Contemporary dark glassmorphism paired with warm Indian Saffron (`#ff9933`), Sunset Terracotta (`#ff5e36`), and Emerald Oasis (`#10b981`).
- **Clean & High-Trust**: Clear source traceability badges, confidence meters, and clean typographic hierarchy.

---

## 2. Color Palette & Visual Tokens
- **Background Primary**: `#0a0e17` (Deep Midnight Slate)
- **Background Secondary**: `#0f172a` (Card Glass Background)
- **Accent Primary (Saffron)**: `#ff9933` (Brand Saffron)
- **Accent Secondary (Terracotta)**: `#ff5e36` (Warm Indian Glow)
- **Accent Success (Emerald)**: `#10b981` (Verified & Nature Spots)
- **Accent Info (Cyan)**: `#06b6d4` (Water & Coastal Stops)

---

## 3. Map Marker Visual Conventions
- **Day 1**: Saffron / Orange (`#ff9933`)
- **Day 2**: Emerald (`#10b981`)
- **Day 3**: Cyan (`#06b6d4`)
- **Day 4+**: Amber / Violet
- **Unassigned Places**: Slate / Purple (`#a855f7`)
- **Confidence High**: Emerald verification badge
- **Confidence Medium / Low**: Amber caution badge with easy "Verify Location" tap
